# ProFox Module 11 Email Copy Rules

This implementation treats the ProFox Brand Messaging & Identity Bible v2.0 as the source of truth for customer-facing copy.

Email rules enforced by the system:
- no emojis
- no em dashes
- calm, intelligent, concise, certain and human voice
- clear action and next step
- no fake urgency
- no inflated agency language
- no unverifiable proof claims
- verified ProFox sender identity stays intact
- human display and Reply-To are used only when the owner identity is valid and safe
- missing personalization data falls back naturally
- transactional CTAs follow the actual task, such as reply, manage booking, pay or review, rather than forcing a marketing CTA into the wrong context

The permanent brand line remains: From site to system.
